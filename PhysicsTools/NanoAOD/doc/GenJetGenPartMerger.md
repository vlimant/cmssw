The `GenJetGenPartMerger` module merges in a single `reco::GenJetCollection` collection, `reco::Genjet` from __srcJet__, after the selection defined by __cut__ and fake `reco::Genjet` produced from `reco::GenParticle` using the 4-vector, pdgId and charge. The identification of a tau in the ancestor of the output jet is carried on from __hasTauAnc__ and false otherwise into a valuemap of boolean.
